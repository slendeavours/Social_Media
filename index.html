The Boardroom
Sessions

Montenegro  ·  Seven days  ·  Ten investors  ·  2026

Most property events are built for an audience. Rows of chairs, a stage, someone selling from the front of the room.

This is ten people around a table for a week. No stage. No sales pitch. The format is structured but deliberately spacious, because the conversations people actually need to have don't happen in 20-minute slots.

We're not filling seats. We're building a room.

The week
Mornings
Working sessions led by people who operate, not people who teach. Strategy, finance, structure, execution.
Afternoons
You bring your own deals and decisions. The room works on them with you.
Guests
Specialists flown in across the week. Finance, legal, sector operators.
Evenings
Nothing scheduled. Time to talk properly over good food, without a clock running.
Included
Accommodation, meals and transfers for everyone offered a place.
Who this is for

The value here is the room. And the room only works if everyone in it is operating at a real level. This isn't a starting point.

You should have active UK property interests, capital you're ready to put to work, and a willingness to put as much into the conversations as you take out of them.

Every application is read. We come back to you either way. Places are offered, not sold.
